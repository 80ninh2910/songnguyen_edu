# BA: Them hoc vien vao lop trung tam da co

## 1. Muc tieu
- Dam bao khi phu huynh dang ky lop o trung tam, he thong khong tu dong tao lop moi.
- Admin chon lop trung tam da co de them hoc vien vao lop do.

## 2. Van de hien tai
- Phu huynh gui form dang ky lop trung tam.
- Khi admin accept yeu cau, he thong tao 1 lop moi.
- Dieu nay gay trung lap lop va sai quy trinh van hanh.

## 3. Business rule
- Neu lop trung tam da duoc tao truoc do, hoc vien moi phai duoc them vao lop da co.
- Khi xu ly yeu cau dang ky, admin bat buoc chon 1 lop trung tam hien co.
- Khong tu dong tao lop moi chi tu viec accept yeu cau.

## 4. Quy trinh de xuat (To-be)
1) Phu huynh gui form dang ky lop trung tam.
2) Yeu cau vao danh sach cho admin xu ly.
3) Admin xem yeu cau va chon lop trung tam da co.
4) He thong them hoc vien vao lop duoc chon.
5) He thong thong bao ket qua cho phu huynh va cap nhat trang thai yeu cau.

## 5. Yeu cau giao dien
- Man hinh xu ly yeu cau can co truong "Chon lop trung tam" (bat buoc).
- Chi hien thi danh sach lop trung tam dang hoat dong.
- Neu khong co lop trung tam phu hop, admin khong the hoan tat xu ly.

## 6. Yeu cau du lieu
- Luu lop trung tam duoc chon vao ban ghi yeu cau.
- Ghi nhan hoc vien da duoc them vao lop nao.

## 7. Ngoai le
- Neu lop da day: hien thong bao va yeu cau admin chon lop khac.
- Neu lop bi dong: khong duoc phep chon.

## 8. Tieu chi chap nhan
- Accept yeu cau khong tao lop moi.
- Hoc vien duoc them vao lop trung tam da chon.
- Bat buoc chon lop khi xu ly yeu cau.
